TacFlight // Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about the TacFlight beta. If you don't find yours here, email us and an aviator on the team will get back to you.

Email the TacFlight team
tacflight.beta@gmail.com

Send an email

A description of the problem and a screenshot are usually enough. Please never send SSNs, DoD ID numbers, or anything classified or CUI.

Joining the beta

How do I join?

On your iPhone, open the TestFlight invite. If you don't have Apple's free TestFlight app yet, the link sends you to the App Store to get it, then back to the invite. Tap Accept, then Install.

When TacFlight opens, create an account with your email and a password and follow the short setup to choose your aircraft and requirements.

Which devices does TacFlight run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.1 or later. Android is in the works. Email us if you'd like to hear when it's ready.

The invite says the beta is full or no longer accepting testers.

Email us and we'll get you in.

How do I get updates?

New beta builds show up in the TestFlight app. Turn on Automatic Updates for TacFlight in TestFlight and you'll always have the latest one.

Each TestFlight build expires 90 days after it's released. Updating to the newest build keeps the app working.

Feedback and bugs

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

The fastest way: take a screenshot in TacFlight and tap Share Beta Feedback. You can also open the TestFlight app, choose TacFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Tell us what you expected, what happened instead, and your aircraft. Screenshots of the screen in question help a lot.

The app crashed. Do I need to do anything?

If TestFlight asks whether to share crash details, tap Share and add a sentence about what you were doing. That goes straight to us.

Your records

Is TacFlight an official Army record?

No. TacFlight is advisory only and helps you keep track of your own hours, currencies and tasks. Always verify your records with your unit.

TacFlight is made by Think Forward Studio LLC and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Army or the Department of Defense.

A currency, minimum or task count looks wrong.

Check the flight's conditions and aircraft first, then send us a screenshot of the card and of the flights it counts. We'd rather hear about a number that looks off than have you work around it.

Can I bring my existing hours in?

Yes. You can import flights from a CSV file (with presets for ARMS and PEX) or a CAFRS export (PDF or Excel), or enter them with Quick Log, several flights at a time.

Your account and data

Where is my logbook stored?

On your device, in an encrypted database, and synced to your account in Google's cloud so it's on all your devices and can be restored. Sync runs automatically while you're signed in.

The privacy policy lists exactly what syncs and what stays on the device.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Log flights offline and they sync the next time you're online. You can also tap Sync Now.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and you'll get an email with a reset link.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install TacFlight from the same TestFlight invite and sign in with the same email. Your logbook and setup come down from your account.

Can I make my own backup?

Yes. Create a backup file in the app and choose a password for it (at least 8 characters). The file is saved on your device and you can share it to somewhere safe. It's encrypted with that password, so keep the password too: we can't open the file without it.

How do I turn off crash reports?

Settings > App > Diagnostics > Share crash reports. Crash reports don't include your flight records or account details.

How do I delete my account?

Tap Delete Account at the bottom of Settings, then confirm your password. Your synced data and your account are deleted. Leave "Also delete local data" ticked to clear the device too. Backup files you created stay until you delete the app.

Signing out doesn't delete anything. It syncs any unsent changes and keeps your data on the device so you can sign back in.
