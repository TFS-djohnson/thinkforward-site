TACFLIGHT
Public beta // iPhone

Logbook and currency tracker for Army aviators

TacFlight puts your ATM semiannual hours, aircraft, NVG and (for AH-64) NVS currency, APART and DA Form 7120-1 tasks on one screen. Built by Army aviators.

iPhone, iOS 16.1 or later. Android is coming.

What's in the beta

Everything you track, counted for you

Log a flight once. TacFlight works out the hours, currencies and tasks it counts toward.

Log by condition

Day, Night, NVG, Hood, Weather, and NVS for AH-64. Quick Log several sorties at once, use Detailed Entry, or import from CSV and CAFRS.

ATM hour minimums

Semiannual and annual minimums, including NVD and simulator hours, plus any higher minimums your unit sets in Unit Minimums.

Currency countdowns

Aircraft, NVG and (for AH-64) NVS currency count down, with fly-by dates and reminders before anything lapses.

APART, task by task

Check off each APART task, and keep annual evaluation and medical certificate windows in view.

DA Form 7120-1 tasks

Keep your tasks for your birth-month ATP year, credited from the flights you log in the modes you flew.

Timer, reports and backups

Time flights with the Live Activity flight timer. Make a flight hours summary PDF, export to CSV, sync between devices and keep encrypted backups.

Why we built it

Built by Army aviators

We're Army aviators who got tired of tracking hours in Notes and on sticky pads. So we built TacFlight: logbook, currency and APART in one app.

It's in open beta, and we need pilots from every airframe to test it before release. Your feedback goes straight to the people writing the code.
