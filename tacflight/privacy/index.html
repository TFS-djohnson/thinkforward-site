TacFlight Privacy Policy

What the TacFlight app collects, where it is kept, who processes it, and how to delete it.

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Describes the TacFlight beta as of Build 32.

Who we are

TacFlight is a flight logbook and currency tracker for Army aviators, made by Think Forward Studio LLC ("we"). This policy covers the TacFlight app and this website section.

What we collect

You need an account to use TacFlight. Everything below is information you enter or import yourself. TacFlight has no fields for your location, contacts, rank, unit name, SSN or DoD ID number. (A form you scan is kept on your device, as described below, and its text can include whatever is printed on the form.)

CategoryWhat it includes
AccountEmail address, a display name (set from your callsign when you sign up), when the account was created and last signed in, and for some older accounts the sign-in method. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
Profile and setupCallsign and display name (which can be your real name), pilot type (military or civilian, each with its own settings), date of birth and birth month, medical type, primary aircraft, local airfield, FAC level, Reserve/National Guard status, waivers, hour requirements and minimums (unit and custom), proration and NVG window settings, available-for-duty date, timer and cockpit modes, and notification and display preferences.
Absence periodsPeriods you record (TDY/deployment, medical suspension, aircraft grounding, aircraft non-availability or leave), with their dates and any notes you add.
Flight recordsDate, aircraft model and tail number, departure and arrival, total time and hours by condition (day, night, NVG, NVS, DS, hood, weather), whether it was a simulator, Hobbs, landings, mission, duty and seat, any remarks you type, and when the record was created and changed.
Training recordsEvaluation, medical and other windows (including BFR, IPC and checkride on a civilian profile, and windows you create), APART components, DA Form 7120-1 tasks and their completions with the flights they came from, and flight templates (name, aircraft, tail number, route, mission, duty, seat and how often you use it).
Beta statusA record that your account is a beta tester, with your user ID and email address.

Please don't enter classified information or CUI in TacFlight, including in notes and remarks.

Kept on your device only

Some information never leaves your device, unless you share it yourself:

  • Scans. When you scan a form, text recognition runs on the device (Google ML Kit). The photo, the recognized text and the photo's technical details (camera model, exposure and similar) are kept on the device and are not uploaded. Location is left out of the details TacFlight records, but the saved photo file can still contain the original photo's embedded details, including location if the photo has it.
  • Aircraft list, Face ID / Touch ID lock setting and usage counters (Settings > About > Usage Stats, where you can reset them).
  • Imported files. CSV, Excel and PDF files you import (including CAFRS, ARMS and PEX formats) are read on the device. The flights you import become flight records and sync like any other.
  • Backup files you create (see Sharing you choose).

How your information is stored and synced

On your device

Your logbook and settings are kept in an encrypted database and encrypted settings storage on the device, using a 256-bit key held in the iOS Keychain (on Android, protected by the Android Keystore). These are stored without that encryption, inside the app's private storage: scan photos, temporary copies of photos and of files you export, backup files (which are encrypted with their own password), and the offline copy that Google Cloud Firestore keeps of your synced data.

Like other app data, this storage can be included in your phone's own backups (for example iCloud).

You can turn on a Face ID, Touch ID or passcode lock. The app asks again after it has been in the background for five minutes. On iPhone, the app always hides its screen in the app switcher. While the flight timer runs, its time shows on your lock screen.

In the cloud

While you're signed in, TacFlight automatically syncs your account, profile and setup, absence periods, flight records, training records, templates and beta status to Google Cloud Firestore, so they're available on your other devices and can be restored. Sync runs when you sign in or out, open the app, come back online, change some settings, or tap Sync Now or Cloud Backup. There is currently no option to use the app without sync.

Synced data is protected by Google's encryption in transit and at rest. The app does not add its own encryption before uploading. Access rules limit each signed-in account to its own data. Think Forward Studio manages beta access and accounts with Firebase's administration tools, which are not limited by those rules.

"Cloud Backup" in the app uses this same synced copy.

Crash reports

TacFlight uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to send reports of crashes and of errors the app logs, so we can fix problems. Reports are on by default. You can turn them off in Settings > App > Diagnostics > Share crash reports, which also deletes reports that haven't been sent yet.

A report contains the type of error and its code (not the error message), a stack trace, recent warning-level app events, your app version and build, and whether you use a military or civilian profile. Crashlytics adds device details such as model, OS version and free memory and storage, uses a Firebase installation identifier and records app sessions. Reports don't include your user ID, email, callsign or records.

TacFlight contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Sharing you choose

  • Exports. PDF reports (which show your callsign, or your name if you have no callsign, and are marked "UNOFFICIAL - Generated by TacFlight") and CSV exports go wherever you send them from the share sheet.
  • Backup files (.tfb) are saved in the app's storage on your device, and you can share them from there. They are encrypted with AES-256 using a password you choose. We can't open them without that password.
  • Feedback. Send Feedback opens your own mail app with an email to the TacFlight team that includes your app version and build, iOS version and device model. Feedback sent through TestFlight includes what you choose to attach, such as a screenshot.

Your choices

  • Turn crash reports on or off in Settings > App > Diagnostics.
  • Allow or deny notifications, camera and photo access in your device settings. Reminders are scheduled on the device; TacFlight doesn't use push notifications.
  • Turn the Face ID / Touch ID lock on or off in Settings > Security.
  • View and change your profile, setup and records at any time in the app.

Deleting your data

Delete your account with the Delete Account button at the bottom of Settings. After you confirm your password, TacFlight deletes your synced records, settings and beta-status record from the cloud and then deletes your sign-in account. If a cloud step fails, your account isn't deleted and you can try again. If beta access was revoked for an account, the record of that (with its email address) is kept.

"Also delete local data" (ticked by default) then removes your records, settings, scan photos and temporary files from that device. If that is interrupted, the app finishes it the next time it opens. It doesn't remove backup files you created or Google Cloud Firestore's offline copy; deleting the app removes those.

Deleting a single record marks it deleted. It disappears from the app right away. Once the deletion has synced, the device copy is removed after 30 days; the cloud copy stays marked as deleted until you delete your account. Scans attached to a deleted flight stay on the device until you delete your account.

Signing out first syncs any unsent changes, then keeps your encrypted data on the device so you can sign back in.

Files you exported or shared, and email you sent us, aren't affected by deleting your account. To ask us to delete anything else, contact us.
